# Retired

The three wordmark directions from before the new palette and type, kept so old slides and screenshots can still be matched. Do not use them in new work: the logo is Twin (see **Logos**).

All are one ink and outlined from Inter.

- `karma-a-wordmark.svg` and `karma-a-mark.svg` — A, Label: KARMA in Inter Bold, tracked 0.18em, and a K cut out of an ink square.
- `karma-b-wordmark.svg` and `karma-b-mark.svg` — B, Citation: [karma] and [k], the square bracket being the citation sign.
- `karma-c-wordmark.svg`, `karma-c-mark.svg` and `karma-c-mark-two-colour.svg` — C, Pulled line: a page with one line pulled out past its edge.
